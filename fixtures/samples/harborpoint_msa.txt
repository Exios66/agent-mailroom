MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2024
by and between HarborPoint Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Client"), and
Northwind Logistics Corporation, a Wisconsin corporation ("Provider").

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree:

1. Services. Provider shall perform logistics and warehousing services described in
each statement of work.

2. Fees. Client shall pay invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3. Term. The initial term is twenty-four (24) months and renews annually unless
either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured
for thirty (30) days after written notice.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written.
