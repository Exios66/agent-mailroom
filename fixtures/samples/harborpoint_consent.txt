UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
OF
HARBORPOINT HOLDINGS, INC.

Pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, the undersigned,
being all of the members of the Board of Directors of HarborPoint Holdings, Inc.,
hereby adopt the following resolutions without a meeting:

RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby approves the entry into a Master
Services Agreement with Northwind Logistics Corporation;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board of Directors hereby establishes the Audit
Committee, consisting of directors Amelia Rodriguez (Chair), Thomas Okafor, and
Priya Nair.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed this Written Consent as of the
10th day of January, 2025.

/s/ Amelia Rodriguez
/s/ Thomas Okafor
/s/ Priya Nair
